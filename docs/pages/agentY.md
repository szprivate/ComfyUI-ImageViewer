# Working with agentY

[Home](../index.md)

With [agentY](https://github.com/szprivate/agentY) installed, the viewer and the agent share their work. None of this shows without it.

- **Grouped by stage**: what agentY saves goes into the Viewer, under a group per stage (*Characters*, *Places*), each thumbnail labelled (a character's name).
- **Tag as asset…** (right-click a thumbnail): name a picture so agentY understands it as `#name`.
- **agentY: approve**: when a review step has stopped a run, select the pictures to keep, right-click, approve (the row is only there while a run waits). The run continues with those.
- **Send to agentY** (Annotate tool): your marks, as a picture and as text, arrive as your next message. During a review it is sent as feedback.
- **The selection**: agentY sees the thumbnails you selected or are comparing. Say "use the selected ones".
- **Show me**: ask agentY to show a file or open the edit, and the viewer brings it up.
- **Dependencies**, **Auto edit**, Paint's **Generate**, **Motion** renders and **Dataset** reviews all go through agentY. See their pages.

## The chat inside the viewer

**ComfyUI ▸ agentY** in the lower bar (or **↗** in the sidebar panel's header) moves agentY's chat into the viewer as a dock panel. It is the same conversation. **↙** or **✕** puts it back.

Drag thumbnails, file browser rows, or (holding <kbd>Shift</kbd>) the frame on screen into the chat to attach them to your next message.
